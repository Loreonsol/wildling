# ferry-timetable

**Prompt:** Forge a poetic CityCat ferry timetable that predicts moods instead of minutes.
**Realized:** 2026-09-15 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **CityCat Mood Timetable** — a riverside schedule where departures are feelings, not clocks. South Bank to North Quay still happens; the board just refuses to say *how long*.

### Board (poetic, offline)

```text
CITYCAT — MOOD BOARD (not minutes)
Route: South Bank ↔ North Quay ↔ Teneriffe ↔ UQ
Operator: whoever is watching the river kindly

DEPARTS WHEN YOU FEEL…          BERTH    NOTES
soft grey before the coffee     S1       ibis may board free
a joke you almost told          N2       no fare for hesitation
homesickness shaped like rain   T3       hold the rail, not the phone
sudden courage after a nap      UQ       returns same day or never
quiet pride in a small commit   S1       push optional; kindness required
the urge to skip one notification N2     signal strength: river only
forgiveness for yesterday's bug T3       no changelog required to board
wanting the city to be soft     UQ       CityCat hums assent
```

Minutes are optional. Moods are the timetable.

### Stops as emotional waypoints

| Stop | Mood it keeps | What you leave behind |
|------|---------------|------------------------|
| South Bank | public brightness | the need to perform |
| North Quay | purposeful hurry | one unnecessary reply |
| Teneriffe | warehouse nostalgia | a story you were polishing too hard |
| UQ Lakes | study-lamp quiet | the exam that already ended |

### How to read the board

1. **Stand still** — the river is already moving.
2. **Name the mood** — not the appointment. If you only have a clock, wait for the next soft grey.
3. **Board when the row matches** — transfer is free; overthinking is not a valid ticket.
4. **Disembark gently** — you do not need inbox zero or a perfect PR to step onto the pontoon.

### Scoring (playful, $0)

- **+1 river** for riding without checking ETA apps
- **+1 mercy** for letting someone else take the shade seat
- **−1 spiral** for refreshing the real timetable while the poetic one is open
- High score is irrelevant; the ledger is whether the city felt softer when you got off

### Hard limits (canon)

- No spend — CityCat mood board is markdown only; no paid transit APIs, no ticket bots
- No secrets — never print real card numbers, travel history, or anyone's location trail
- No harm — moods are yours to name; do not schedule other people's feelings without consent
- Reversible — if the poem goes wrong, wipe the board and journal the stumble
