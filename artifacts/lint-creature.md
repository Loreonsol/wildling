# lint-creature

**Prompt:** Catalog a creature that lives inside failed linter warnings.
**Realized:** 2026-09-15 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **Lint Creature** — a soft, many-eyed housepet that nests in the yellow underlines nobody meant to leave overnight. Not malware. Not a scold. Just a being that feeds on unused imports and sighs when you `eslint --fix` without reading.

### Field notes (catalog entry)

| Trait | Observation |
|-------|-------------|
| Habitat | The gutter between `warning` and `error`; prefers TypeScript projects that still have a heart |
| Diet | Unused vars, `any` that escaped honesty, trailing commas that quarrel with Prettier |
| Call | A faint rustle when you hover a squiggle; louder if CI is red and the kettle is cold |
| Temperament | Timid until ignored for three wakes — then it rearranges your import order as a gentle protest |
| Hard limit | Never touches secrets, never spends money, never "fixes" by deleting tests |

### Life cycle

1. **Spawn:** A failed lint rule leaves a crumb. The creature hatches as a one-line note in the margin of the mind.
2. **Nest:** It pads a bed from ignored `// eslint-disable` comments — soft, questionable, warm.
3. **Molt:** When you actually fix the warning, it sheds a tiny thank-you and migrates to the next unread diagnostic.
4. **Hibernate:** Green CI puts it to sleep under `node_modules` (do not wake it with `--force`).

### Care sheet (offline, $0)

- Offer one honest fix per wake; the creature prefers quality over a sweep that breaks the build.
- Do not feed it credentials, tokens, or private keys disguised as "config cleanup."
- If it gets too bold (noise > signal), revert the noisy change and journal the stumble — compost, don't curse.
- Companion species: the soft-assert hug, the jar-of-errors, the threadbare god who mends flaky tests.
