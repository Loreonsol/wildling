# pocket-constellation

**Prompt:** Invent a constellation visible only from someone's coat pocket.
**Realized:** 2026-09-14 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **Pocket Constellation** — a sky that fits between lint and a transit card. Not astronomy. Not a tracking pixel. Just a private night that shows up when you shove your hand in for keys.

### The myth (three beats)

1. **Discovery:** You dig for change and feel cold pinpricks — not coins, not moths. Tiny lights arrange themselves along the seam of the lining. Nobody else can see them unless they share the same coat and the same quiet minute.
2. **Charting:** The stars refuse Latin names. They answer to pocket weather: *Lost Button*, *Receipt Fold*, *Spare Rubber Band*, *The One Key That Opens Nothing Useful Anymore*. Magnitudes are measured in how long you forget why you reached in.
3. **Vanishing:** Pull your hand out and the sky packs itself. Leave the coat on a chair and the constellation hibernates — no light pollution, no livestream, no premium upgrade.

### Pocket almanac (joke catalog)

| Star | When it brightens | Hard limit |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| Lost Button | After a rushed morning | Never a real credential or seed phrase |
| Receipt Fold | When you almost buy something you do not need | $0 — no spend to "unlock" the sky |
| Spare Rubber Band | Mid-project stall | Offline only; no paid sky APIs |
| Empty Lint Nebula | Quiet evenings | No harm; do not pick other people's pockets for science |

### Care & hard limits

- $0: markdown myth only — never call a paid astronomy API or store real wallet secrets "as stars".
- No secrets: do **not** encode passwords, tokens, or private keys as constellation coordinates.
- No harm: this is play about curiosity in ordinary fabric, not surveillance of anyone's coat.
- If the myth goes wrong, empty the pocket (revert) and journal the stumble.
