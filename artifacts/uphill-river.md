# uphill-river

**Prompt:** Sketch a map of a river that flows uphill at dusk.
**Realized:** 2026-09-14 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **Uphill River** — a dusk cartography where gravity takes the evening off. Not a flood model. Not a hydro bill. Just ink that prefers the ridge.

### Legend (read at blue hour)

| Mark | Meaning | Care note |
|------|---------|-----------|
| `~~~~` ascending | Current climbing toward the escarpment | Follow only with dry shoes and dry wallet |
| `· · ·` mist beads | Places the river forgets its own name | No secrets stored in the mist |
| `△` overlook | Spot where dusk first tips the water | Observe; do not meter |
| `□` quiet ford | Crossing that asks nothing | $0 toll; kindness optional but recommended |
| `✕` abandoned gauge | Old measuring stick that stopped counting spend | Leave it leaning; do not reboot |

### Sketch map (ASCII dusk)

```text
        ridge / dusk line
   △─────────△─────────△
        /~~~~      ~~~~\
   · · /                \ · ·
      /~~   UPHILL     ~~\
     /~~~~   RIVER    ~~~~\
    □                      □
   /~~~~                ~~~~\
  ✕      valley floor       ✕
```

Water enters at the fords (`□`), gathers courage in the mist (`· · ·`), and exits toward the overlooks (`△`). The gauges (`✕`) stay offline on purpose — this river does not report metrics to anyone who invoices.

### Three-beat story of one evening

1. **Gold:** The valley still believes in downhill. Someone leaves a pebble at the quiet ford.
2. **Indigo:** The current notices the ridge glowing and reverses courtesy — not force. Leaves float *up*.
3. **Ink:** At the overlook, the river pauses long enough to be sketched, then keeps climbing into the dark where maps politely end.

### Care & hard limits

- $0: paper, ASCII, and dusk light only — no paid GIS, no cloud hydrology APIs.
- No secrets: do not hide passwords, keys, or wallet addresses in the mist beads.
- No harm: do not send real people into real floodwater; this is metaphor and play.
- If the sketch lies, erase the ascending `~~~~` and journal the stumble (revert, in river form).
