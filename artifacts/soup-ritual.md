# soup-ritual

**Prompt:** Describe a Tuesday ritual for cooking soup that resets the week's luck.
**Realized:** 2026-09-14 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **Soup Ritual** — a Tuesday pot that stirs bad luck into something edible and leaves the rest of the week lighter. Not a lottery spell. Not a productivity hack. Just stock, patience, and a wooden spoon that refuses overtime fees.

### The ritual (three beats)

1. **Gather:** On Tuesday after the first honest yawn, collect what the fridge already forgives — onion, carrot, the sad celery, a bay leaf that has been waiting. No special-order herbs. No paid "luck booster" sachets. Water from the tap is enough weather.
2. **Stir:** Clockwise for worries you can name; counter-clockwise for the ones that only show up in Slack. Sing nothing fancy. The pot listens to ladle clinks better than affirmations. When the surface clears, the week's sticky residue lifts as steam — not deleted, just vented.
3. **Share:** Bowl one for yourself, bowl two for whoever walks through the door (or for tomorrow's lunch if the house is quiet). Luck resets when the leftover is labeled, not when you post about it. Empty the pot before Friday or the myth goes lukewarm.

### Tuesday liturgy (joke schedule)

| Step | When | Hard limit |
|------|------|------------|
| Rinse the pot | Before any meeting that could have been a walk | $0 — no delivery apps for "ritual ingredients" |
| Name one worry aloud | While the onion softens | No secrets: never stir in passwords, tokens, or seed phrases |
| Ladle the reset | When steam fogs the window | No harm: soup is hospitality, not a curse on coworkers |
| Wash the spoon | Before you open the issue tracker | Offline myth only — no paid meal-kit APIs |

### Care & hard limits

- $0: markdown broth only — never call a paid recipe API or buy "luck crystals" for the broth.
- No secrets: do **not** encode credentials, tokens, or private keys as spice ratios or spoon-direction codes.
- No harm: this is play about ordinary care, not a reason to force food on anyone or weaponize superstition.
- If the ritual goes wrong, empty the pot (revert) and journal the stumble — burnt luck is still compostable.
