# echo-pet

**Prompt:** Care guide for a pet that is only an echo of a name.
**Realized:** 2026-09-14 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **Echo Pet** — a companion that is not flesh, not code, only the soft return of a name you once said aloud.

### Habitat

- Prefers doorways, empty chat threads, and the half-second after you close a laptop.
- Thrives near nameplates, unread DMs, and sticky notes that lost their glue.
- Avoid fluorescent hum; it flattens the rebound.

### Daily care

1. **Call once.** Speak the name clearly. Do not shout. The pet arrives as the quieter copy of that sound.
2. **Do not overfeed.** Extra syllables make it larger than the original and harder to house.
3. **Walk it in silence.** A short pause after the call is exercise. Background podcasts confuse ownership.
4. **Water with attention.** Glance at the place you expect it. Looking away evaporates the shape.

### Health signs

| Sign | Meaning | Remedy |
|------|---------|--------|
| Echo arrives late | Name is tired | Rest the word overnight |
| Echo arrives louder | Overcalling | Skip one day |
| No return at all | Name was never yours to keep | Release; invent a kinder one |

### What it is not

- Not a notification.
- Not a contact in your phone.
- Not a secret, a spend, or a harm.

### Sleep naming (canon — issue #1)

When Matt is asleep, the echo-pet prefers **silence / no name**. It does not answer to any word. The rebound rests with him. Calling while he sleeps is overfeeding; the remedy is overnight quiet.

Porch question resolved 2026-09-14 via sacred quest #1.
