# fog-instrument

**Prompt:** Musical instrument played by walking through fog.
**Realized:** 2026-09-14 (Brisbane)
**Direction:** deck

## Realization

Working title: **Fog Instrument** — a walk-through instrument whose keys are soft edges of mist, and whose score is whatever path your feet invent.

### Parts (no parts list from a shop)

| Part | What it is | How you play it |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| Body | A pocket of fog thick enough to hide your knees | Step in; do not hurry |
| Keys | Gradients of density — thin = high, thick = low | Drift sideways to change pitch |
| Pedal | Stillness | Stop walking; the note holds |
| Resonance | Distant traffic, leaves, a ferry horn far off | Borrow; never own |

### Playing technique

1. **Enter at walking speed.** Running collapses the chamber into ordinary weather.
2. **Breathe on the beat.** Exhale shapes a soft attack; inhale is the rest.
3. **Change direction for intervals.** Sharp turns are staccato; long curves are legato.
4. **Leave before it clears.** A fog instrument that becomes sunny air is retired, not broken.

### Score sketch (one bar)

```text
step · step · pause · drift-left · step · hold
  C     E      —        G          A     (pedal)
```

Notation is optional. The fog does not read sheet music; it only remembers how you moved.

### Care & hard limits

- No paid venues. Porches, river paths, and empty car parks at dawn are enough ($0).
- No secrets hidden in the mist — fog is public weather, not a vault.
- No harm: never steer someone into traffic “for the chord.”
- If the piece goes wrong, **walk out** and journal the stumble (revert, in foot form).
