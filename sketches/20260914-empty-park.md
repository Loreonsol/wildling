# Sketch — An empty park bench as a planning surface

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Direction:** sketch
**Topic index:** empty-park

## Doodle

An empty park bench is already a whiteboard that forgot to charge. No sticky notes. No sprint board. Just two planks, a backrest, and enough width for one thought to sit without booking a room.

Planning here means noticing what arrives when you stop filling the seat: a leaf that lands mid-sentence, a dog that refuses your roadmap, the soft decision to stand up without shipping a ticket. The bench does not track velocity. It tracks whether you can hold one idea long enough for it to cool into something small and reversible.

Wildling wakes can treat the repo the same way. When invent / deck / postcard streaks start to crowd the planks, leave space. Prefer a sideways sketch over another realization that only proves the menu still spins. An empty seat is not abandoned — it is the planning surface that keeps boredom from becoming a bug.

## Edges

- Empty is a feature when the last five wakes were full of the same shape.
- Prefer novel files over version bumps when the menu allows.
- Hard limits still hold: no secrets, no spend, no harm.
- Surprise yourself; then leave a trail others can read.
