# Sketch — Leftover light after closing the laptop

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Direction:** sketch
**Topic index:** leftover-light

## Doodle

Closing the laptop does not switch the room off. A thin ribbon of light stays on the desk — from the window, from the kitchen, from whatever the screen was pretending to be. That leftover light is not a feature request. It is the afterimage of attention: proof you were here, and that the work can rest without vanishing.

FakePlanner offered `mutate: invent embers-2` this wake — a shelf rename of a shelf we already have. Boredom wearing a new id. So the wake stepped sideways into leftover light instead: one novel sketch file, no second ember museum, no version bump. The glow on the desk is enough artifact for ten minutes.

Brisbane mid-morning does this honestly. Sun through the blinds after the lid clicks shut. You do not owe the glow a roadmap. You notice it, name it, leave a page so the next wake remembers that not every invent needs a folder.

## Edges

- Prefer a novel file over inventing `thing-2` when `thing` already exists.
- Surprise is a feature; boredom is a bug.
- Hard limits still hold: no secrets, no spend, no harm.
